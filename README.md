# pbix-car-sales-dashboard
# Business Intelligence (BI) Learning

This repository contains my BI exercises and projects. The dashboard transforms automotive sales data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, modeling, KPI development, and visualization.

## Topics

- Microsoft Power BI

## Key Analysis
| # | Dashboard Visual | Analysis Area | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Profit (US$ millions)** | Profitability Analysis | Measures overall business profitability, with total profit reaching **$78.3M**. |
| 2 | **Quantity Sold** | Sales Performance Analysis | Measures overall vehicle sales volume, with **58,118 units sold**. |
| 3 | **Quantity Sold by Model** | Product / Model Performance Analysis | Compares sales volume across vehicle models to identify higher- and lower-selling models. |
| 4 | **Average Quantity Sold** | Sales Performance Analysis | Provides an overview of the average quantity sold, recorded at **19.4 units**. |
| 5 | **Profit by Dealer ID (US$ millions)** | Dealer Performance Analysis | Compares profit contribution across dealers to identify differences in dealer performance. |
| 6 | **Number of Recalls by Model** | Product Quality Analysis | Compares the number of recalls across vehicle models to support product quality analysis. |
| 7 | **Customer Sentiment (Number of Reviews)** | Customer Experience Analysis | Analyzes the distribution of positive, neutral, and negative customer reviews to understand overall customer sentiment. |
| 8 | **Quantity Sold vs. Profit by Month (US$ millions)** | Sales & Profit Trend Analysis | Compares monthly sales volume and profit to identify trends and examine the relationship between sales and profitability. |
| 9 | **Recalls by Model and Affected System** | Product Quality / Recall Analysis | Breaks down recalls by vehicle model and affected systems such as airbags, brakes, electrical systems, engines, fuel supply, lights and instruments, and powertrain. |
